Countdown audio check

Tap the buttons in order. Turn the volume up first, and note which way the ringer switch on the side of the phone is set.

The ringer switch mutes Web Audio but not a media element, which is why 4 is audible on silent and 3 is not. These two are the candidate fixes. Set the ringer to silent, tap one, then tap 3 again and see whether it is audible.
